# X11b — Refresh guard, recovery and concern-level duplication

**PLAN ONLY — not preregistered; separate council track.** Owner decision2026-11-08 split X11a assignment accuracy from later guard/recovery evidence. Council round5 R5-08, adopted under R-064 and recorded in ADR-013, additionally leaves **REQ-176 concern-level pending duplication unmeasured in X11a** and outstanding here. This plan authorises no data collection, code execution, map, labelling or analysis.

## Retained scope

Start from the product guard (20 merges, 20 clusters, fallback limit .40, pending-new limit .20), current refresh-generation filtering, product eligible merge slots, decision-time ordering, whole-merge fallback completeness and latched first fire. Directory guard is not applicable; Jev-only pending-new is unmeasured. Compare precision and coverage-based staleness (recall fall/gold-gap increase), not catalogue age alone. Guard results remain a small descriptive case series, not validation that the guard works generally.

A future preregistration must fix: statistical healthy/fall references; shared guard-slot clock; warning horizon H; mutually exclusive timely/false/late/censored categories; initial-unhealthy handling; full follow-up/censoring; and stochastic calibration including healthy high-escalation stress streams. R1-03 and R1-14–16 from [X11 round 1](../x11-merge-association/reviews/round-1.md) move here. R2-06 requires a small-sample-valid reference interval (not a four-block degenerate bootstrap) and reporting the share establishing a healthy reference. R2-07 requires recovery eligibility and follow-up on the same clock, sufficient assignment counts and an explicit midpoint comparator. These are **open design requirements**, not settled rules imported unreviewed from X11a.

Recovery must compare refreshed-minus-same-commit-no-refresh precision/recall over matched follow-up bounds, with cluster-robust uncertainty and different residual populations disclosed. Only an eligible fall can be called recovery; an intervention after a false warning is separate. Genuine `scout map --refresh` uses the pinned mapper/reconciliation, no gold membership repair. New stochastic map draws confound the mechanism. Fix whether midpoint intervention always runs and its follow-up before data. Preserve own prior assignments, pending out of catalogue and a preregistered maintainer policy.

**Concern-level duplication remains unmeasured.** X11a's random12-case illustration cannot establish how many pending areas represent each real new concern, so it no longer reports k, duplicate rates or duplicate-loss estimates. A future X11b preregistration must fix coverage of all relevant pending-origin clusters, a feasible context/grouping budget, and k **within each primary-arm×catalogue-age state**, deduplicating cluster identity rather than summing states. It must distinguish missed concerns, duplicate pending IDs, pre-existing gaps and genuinely new concerns, with explicit unmeasured counts/uncertainty. No random-case zero count may stand in for this REQ-176 outcome. Sources: R-076, [X11a council round5](../x11-merge-association/reviews/round-5.md#2-new-issues), [ADR-013](../../docs/adrs/2026-11-08-x11a-delegated-decisions/ADR.md).
