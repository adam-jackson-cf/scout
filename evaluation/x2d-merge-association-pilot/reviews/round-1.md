- **Cause.** n, not the model: at n = 63 the +0.10 point-gain condition under δ = 0.15 passes ≈ 83 % of the time and the 0.90 `P_assign` point ≈ 96 %; product ≈ 0.78 (1/1000 INCONCLUSIVE, so the bounds do not bind). Raising δ, raising ρ, pooling refinement rounds or softening the user-accepted GO rule are rejected as goalpost moves.

The spare's only other use (replacing a non-informative round) was never implemented and is low-value once R1-04 makes real rounds ≥ the pre-pass sizes; a non-informative frozen round is INCONCLUSIVE (R1-19), which is the honest outcome.
