### R10-01 — blocks sign-off: on the real catalogue, P1 removes an area's **only** file, so two P1 items have a gold area that is not in `catalogue-p1`

**Finding (experiment-peer; verified by the lead against the frozen inputs, membership only).** `_filter_catalogue` keeps an area only while it has a file left (`build_controls.py:162-165`); `catalogue-p1` = the catalogue minus the P1 members (`:258-261`); the response reports `catalogue-p1` at **113** of 114 areas. No area of `catalogue.json` is empty, so exactly one area lost all its files to P1 — and since every P1 unit's members are owned solely by its gold area (`_unique_owner`, `:189-196`), that area is the gold of a P1 item. The area is identified: `26ac01437eaf` owns exactly one file, `scripts/playwright/request_form_models/image01.py` (`catalogue.json`, the only ≤ 2-file area whose files are all P members); **two** P clusters consist of exactly that file, `2c4db77221b8e719` and `af2f04c8c181c116` (two commits; `prepass/reserve.json`), both eligible, both placeable, and both in P1 by the hash order the response's donor list confirms (placeable[0] = `2c4db772`, placeable[19] = `af2f04c8`, placeable[20:24] = the four recorded P-leftover donors). So two P1 items carry `gold.acceptable = ["26ac01437eaf"]` while `catalogue-p1` has no such area.

**Why it meets the bar.** The P1 gate counts a hit only when V-sem's `area_id` is in the key gold's `acceptable` (`analysis_x2d.py:1357`); an area absent from the catalogue Jev is given cannot be answered, so both items are guaranteed misses and the gate silently becomes "≥ 12 of 18" (0.667) instead of the preregistered "≥ 12 of 20" (0.60). A gate FAIL stops the pilot before round 1 (`PREREGISTRATION.md:108`), so a construction artefact could end the pilot. The construct is also broken for those items: the P1 test is "recover A from content despite the path cue", which presupposes A exists. Labellers see the same catalogue and cannot label A either. The defect is in rev 9's allocation (the memo's `spare/moved` check and the fixture did not cover it, and the stub world has no one-file area whose file is a P unit); it is ruled on now because the round-0 real build is the first time it binds and because its fix changes the step-6 record this round was asked to verify.

**Fix (all three parts; `PREREGISTRATION.md` rev 11, `build_controls.py`, `validation/validate.py`):**
1. `build_controls.py`, placeable loop (`:263-270`): compute `c1 = _filter_catalogue(cat, removed | set(r["members"]))` first; if `a` is not an `area_id` of `c1["areas"]` (A would lose its last owned file), append `(r, a)` to `unplaceable` and `continue`; otherwise build `o` from `c1` as now.
