# X2d — semantic association of merged changes with areas (fastapi 2025-01 → 2026-10 pilot)

Question: can a Jev extension of the Clawpatch agent mapper — scoped onboarding draws consolidated in code, then each merged PR's residual change clusters associated with the frozen catalogue by a `covered` Noul read before a permuted whole-catalogue Choice — assign the clusters it resolves to the right area (`P_assign ≥ 0.90`, `R_assign ≥ 0.80` over resolved clusters), abstaining on at most 15 %, and clearly beat a directory baseline (B2) on the same resolved clusters, with new-area detection (`P_new`, `R_new`) reported with intervals and no veto (user decision 2026-10-30, R-051)? Everything is in `PREREGISTRATION.md` (revision 9, after council round 8 `reviews/round-8.md`, draft for council round 9); the governing memo is `reviews/shape-decision.md`; `DRAFT-NOTES.md` has the draft's digests, judgement calls and open items. **Nothing here has been scored: no Jev call, no label, no arm output exists.** The files marked *exists* below were produced by the model-free pre-pass and one Clawpatch measurement map; everything else is the implementer's contract.

The guide and the question files of a round are `data/rounds/<R>/inputs/{GUIDE.md,questions/*.json}` (created at the freeze: rounds 0–1 from the study directory, later rounds from the previous round's inputs unless the drafter's proposal already sits there); the study-directory files are the frozen v_0 base. `label` first renders the exact Jev states with `jev -dump-state` (no key, no request; with the effective instructions) and then labels (wide, perception on `states.jsonl`, and in rounds 1 and 10 the contrast window sample); `label` forwards only `--new-round`, `--workers` and `--cases`; `labels-check R` writes `labels-check.json` (`ok` = κ of the joint answer over the GO population ≥ 0.6 alone; the rule concentration is reported only) and exits 1 only for a refinement round with no recorded guide revision and no `rounds/R/clarify-refused.json` (round 0, rounds 8–10 and a revised or refused round exit 0), before any Jev score exists; `gold R` writes gold and the next membership together or not at all (a window unit without gold: fill it with `label --new-round NAME`), and `run.sh gold` / `gates-label` call `gold.py` for the main gold and the contrast gold (round 0: its two files) separately, skipping a file that already exists, so a contrast gold completed after the main gold exists succeeds (R4-01); `baselines` run after gold exists and are replayed per PR. A stage refuses when its outputs exist (append-only), when labels already exist for a changed unit set, when a digest of a frozen file differs from the sign-off record (or the frozen file set differs from it), or (`jev`) when the cumulative billed cost exceeds the cap. `X2D_SKIP_SIGNOFF` and `X2D_REVIEWS_DIR` are validation-only: honoured only with a loopback `X2D_JEV_ENDPOINT` and an `X2D_DATA` outside `S/data`.

**Operating characteristics of the GO rule (read before any spend).** The GO rule (PREREGISTRATION §9; user decision 2026-10-30, R-051) judges `P_assign`, `R_assign_res` and the paired gain over B2 on the clusters Jev resolves, caps the unresolved rate at 0.15, guards against abstention gaming with the one-sided 95 % PR-cluster-bootstrap lower bound of `gain_guess` (abstentions answered by Jev's own unthresholded guess; must be > 0) and reports new-area detection with intervals and no veto. A NO-GO on the cap is a measured product failure, not evidence that Jev's answers are wrong; an INCONCLUSIVE on the guard says Jev shows no accuracy edge over B2 once its abstentions are answered by its own unthresholded guess (the resolved-cluster gain may be selection); a self-aware Jev with no accuracy edge over B2 is treated as a null by council decision, because Scout has no fallback for an unresolved association and X2d changes no design; about 10 gold-`none` positives cannot veto. The binding power cells are P3-u0 ≥ 0.80, P3-u05 ≥ 0.75, P3-u10 ≥ 0.60 and N3, N3-PR, N4a, N4b, N4b-cap, N4b-rho0 ≤ 0.10 (`data/power.json`, schema `x2d-power-v5`); they are conditional simulations, not the power of the real pilot, and the measured cells are in `reviews/round-6-response.md` §4 (the full measured table, R6-07). A P3 cell below its bar on the live structure -> user decision; a null cell above its bar -> stop. A false P3 cell, or a frozen round with < 20 clusters or < 6 PRs, needs a recorded `user_decision: proceed` in the round-0 critique; a false null cell is a problem no line can clear. In a validation run a binding cell on the wrong side of its bar is a FAILED check.
