## 1. Question and hypothesis

Scout maps a repository with the Clawpatch agent mapper at onboarding and on manual `scout map` only (R-043). Between maps, merged changes bring new files; today nothing associates them with areas by meaning. **HD6**: a Jev extension — onboarding consolidation of *scoped* agent draws (round 0) plus merge-time association of each merged PR's **residual change clusters** with the frozen catalogue by a `covered` Noul read before a permuted whole-catalogue Choice — assigns the clusters it resolves to the right area with `P_assign ≥ 0.90` and `R_assign ≥ 0.80` (over resolved clusters), abstaining (unresolved) on ≤ 15 % of clusters, and beats a directory baseline (B2) on the same resolved clusters by ≥ +0.10, and, over all clusters, its answers — a resolved cluster by its composed answer, an abstained cluster by its own unthresholded guess — beat B2 (one-sided 95 % lower bound of `gain_guess` > 0), so the gain is not wholly an artefact of where it abstains, on fastapi merges 2025-01 → 2026-10; new-area detection (`P_new`, `R_new`) is not part of HD6: it is the separate hypothesis **HD6b** (`proposed`, not tested here), and is reported with intervals as descriptive figures and cannot veto (about 10 cases; user decision 2026-10-30, R-051, §14; R5-06); GO on frozen rounds 8–10 (§9) means "worth a full trial". Questions for the design: (Q1) GO / INCONCLUSIVE / NO-GO; (Q2) which questions, state fields and variants (V-sem, V-sib, V-title) the refinement rounds converged on; (Q3) whether the onboarding extension yields a usable catalogue (round-0 report).
Out of scope: any other repository, any Clawpatch or model change, area titling by a model (new areas are left unowned and pooled, user decision 4), splits/merges of areas, the destructive `Reconcile` refresh (shape-decision §0.c; a GO needs a design round for membership layers, a frozen `summary` field and many-to-many lineage), cost beyond the §12 caps.

### 4.2 Catalogue construction (`onboard/main.go`, code first; Jev for contested files only)
1. Read each draw with `clawpatch.LoadFeatures`, keep `IsAgent` features (`FeatureFiles`).

## 7. The narrow Jev questions (frozen files `questions/*.json`)
Composition (`compose.py`): `merge.covered` (Noul) → if `1 − p_true < s` (i.e. `p_true > t_cov`; equality is `none`) then `merge.which_area` (Choice over the whole catalogue, permuted order `permutation:unit_id`, 2–255 options) → **assign** if the top option's confidence ≥ `t_choice` (and, for the 20 % of clusters asked under a second permutation — `x2d.flip_selected(cluster_id)` or the run's `flip_all`, recorded in the scores header — both permutations agree; a missing required row is `unresolved:failure`, R2-13) else **unresolved** (reported, not counted as an assignment); if `1 − p_true ≥ s` (`p_true ≤ t_cov`) → **none** → the pool. `t_cov = 1 − s`: a cluster is `none` iff `1 − p_true ≥ s`, with `s` the tuned score threshold (stored); for `merge.covered` a tuned 0.5 is replaced by the next grid value *below* in score space, for the other decisions by the next above. Every V-sem row also carries the **unthresholded** answer, `raw_covered` (`p_true ≥ 0.5` on `merge.covered`) and `raw_choice` (the permutation-1 top option), which the perception check (§8) and the guard h (§9, on abstained clusters; fan-out rows carry them too) read. "New area" is the `false` branch of the Noul, never a Choice option. Both thresholds are tuned (§9), never 0.5. Option criteria text is `"{title}: {summary} Sample files: …"`, opaque ids as options; candidate coverage of existing areas is 1.0 by construction (no file gate, no top-K).
State (named JSON, redacted by the existing `redactAll`/`Redact` path): `unit {paths ≤ 10, change_kind: added | renamed_from <path>, role, excerpt ≤ 60 lines of the representative member}`, `pr {title, body ≤ 1,500 chars, changed_paths with status}` (title and body marked untrusted in the instructions), `diff_summary {files, +/- lines, ≤ 8 top changed symbols}`, `catalogue[]` (the Noul only; the Choice carries the catalogue in its options). Model: `typesafe/jev-1.14-20261014` (`internal/policy.DefaultModel`) through Scout's client; the question version key (`decisions.Question.Version`) is bound at run time for each variant and catalogue digest, and the question as sent is written to `data/questions/<id>@<sha256>.json`. The exact state a model sees (field order, embedded catalogue, Choice option texts and criteria, redaction, `sample_files[:n]`, `include_pr_title`) **and the instructions and criteria it is asked with** (the question's base text plus the variant's `instructions_delta`, R2-17) are rendered by `jev -dump-state` (no key, no request) into `states.jsonl`; perception and reference sessions read that file and never a re-derivation (R1-16).

## 8. Oracle and labels

The user does not review each case (user decision 2026-10-23). **Labels are appended, never edited.** `label_x2d.py` is the X2 `label.py` adapted to two questions (it must not import X2's frozen `GUIDE.md` or labels).

**Controls, per round (R1-07)** (ids `<r>:ctl-<n>`, indistinguishable from real units, `control: true`, excluded from every metric and from absorption): each round's `units.jsonl` adds ceil(10 %) hash-chosen duplicate units (test–retest), one empty-file rename trap and one misleading-PR-prose clone. They are the test–retest evidence for the critique's "label" cause. **Perception check** (`error-analysis.md`; R2-09, R2-17): ≥ 20 window units (never controls) per version where both labellers also answer Jev's exact two questions **from Jev's exact V-sem state** (`states.jsonl`, with the effective instructions and criteria the driver sends, base plus the V-sem `instructions_delta`; the prompt carries no guide; 20 units × 2 questions × 2 labellers = 80 sessions). The concentration is **report-only** (`rule_top_share_over_0_40`), never a trigger: the guide's general rules (R1, responsibility first; R2, tests) are cited by construction, so a concentration trigger would fail every round (R3-02); `ok` is decided by κ of the joint answer ≥ 0.6 alone. `labels_sha256` is the SHA-256 of the sorted JSON of the current-guide wide rows of the checked units: `jev`, `gold`, `tune` and `analysis` recompute it and refuse on a mismatch (after `label --new-round NAME` run `labels-check R` again) and refuse without a labels-check of the current guide version (round 0's Jev too). **Round 0:** the check is report-only (it still writes the file; `require_labels_check 0` checks existence, guide version and digest only); K4 on the D units (§5) is the round-0 κ gate. **Rounds 1–7:** a failed check (κ < 0.6) refuses `jev`/`gold`/`tune`/`analysis` only while neither a guide revision nor `rounds/R/clarify-refused.json` is recorded, so the one guide clarification (§10 (v)) runs before scoring; after the recorded clarification, or after a refused one (below), a still-failing κ lets the round proceed, scored and **non-informative** (→ §9). **Refused clarification (R4-02, R5-12):** when the pre-check of `label_x2d.py --precheck-clarify` (run by `run.sh clarify-precheck` and again by `run.sh clarify`) refuses because relabelling the round's wide cases does not fit the session cap, that pre-check writes `rounds/R/clarify-refused.json` (`{utc, reason, cap, used, planned}`, create-once, never overwritten) and exits non-zero; `labels-check` and `require_labels_check` treat that file like a recorded revision, so a round that must clarify and cannot is not stuck. **Cap breach (R4-02):** a round whose wide labels cannot be completed within its session cap, also after a recorded clarification, is an integrity pause "cap breach": the round stops, it is reported, and the council and the user decide in a new review round; it is not evidence against HD6 and is not counted towards futility (§9). Rounds 8–10 cannot clarify the guide: a failed labels-check there is recorded, the round is scored and marked **non-informative** (→ INCONCLUSIVE, §9). Report κ on **all** scored units of each round (X2c's κ covered a subset) and, for informativeness and futility, on the GO population (R2-14), adjudication rate and rule-citation concentration per round. A round is **informative** only if it has ≥ 20 scored clusters from ≥ 6 PRs, κ of the **joint answer** ≥ 0.6 (none / area with acceptable-overlap mapped to one label) over the GO population (core-gate units excluded) and a labels-check that has not failed; κ on `covered`, raw agreement and prevalence are reported and decide nothing (κ is degenerate at high prevalence).

## 9. Metrics, minimums, the go/no-go rule
**Per cluster outcome:** `assigned(a)`, `none`, `unresolved` (Jev abstention, B0/B2 tie, disagreement of two permutations, a missing required permutation row after a cost stop: `unresolved:failure`). A cluster is **resolved** when the primary arm's composed output is `assigned` or `none`, and **abstained** when it is `unresolved` for any reason (`below_t_cov` / `below_t_choice`, `permutation_disagreement`, `failure`; a cost stop or a missing row can never hide an error). B2's own `unresolved` (a B0/B2 tie) stays what it was: a measured wrong answer for B2; B2 never abstains in this sense. Correct iff `assigned ∧ a ∈ acceptable` or `none ∧ gold none`. `P_assign` = correct ÷ assigned-to-any-area (a wrong assignment on a gold-`none` positive counts as assigned); **`R_assign_res`** = assigned-correct ÷ (gold-covered ∧ resolved) (a false `none` on a covered cluster is a resolved wrong answer and counts here; this is the `R_assign` of the minimums and of GO condition c); `R_assign` on **all** gold-covered clusters (abstention = miss) is reported beside it; the **unresolved rate** `u` = abstained ÷ GO population; `P_new` = `none` ∧ gold none ÷ `none`; `R_new` = `none` ∧ gold none ÷ gold none (descriptive, with Wilson 95 %: real and planted positives reported separately; gold `none` split into gap vs new); paired gain = (Jev correct − B2 correct) per cluster on the same clusters (exact McNemar + PR-cluster bootstrap), `gain_res` on the resolved clusters and `gain_all` on all of them (below); per catalogue stratum and per role; confusion matrix over area pairs (errors concentrated on near-duplicate areas are attributed to the catalogue). Controls and `core_gate` units are excluded from the GO figures. Per round `modified_unowned` (pooled / never owned) is reported as a coverage figure; the round's rule counts are persisted (`rounds/<r>/counts.json`), `lookups.jsonl` carries a row for every commit with any non-zero rule count, and the end-to-end figures assert that the rows sum to `counts.json` (R2-07). Intervals: Wilson 95 % per round; pooled via PR-cluster bootstrap (B = 10,000, seed 20261029).
**Minimums (assumed; user accepted the council defaults, decision 6):** `P_assign ≥ 0.90`, `R_assign ≥ 0.80` (`x2d.MINIMUMS`; in the GO rule `R_assign` means `R_assign_res`, over resolved clusters). The unresolved-rate cap `u ≤ 0.15` (`UNRESOLVED_RATE_MAX`) and the data minimum `n_res ≥ 50` resolved paired clusters (`RESOLVED_MIN`) are the council's settings under the user's decision of 2026-10-30 (R-051, §14). `P_new`, `R_new` are **not** minimums of the verdict: they are reported with intervals; the former 0.80 bound survives only as the round-0 planted-new / P2 gate bound (`NEW_AREA_GATE_MIN`, §5). The costlier error is a **silent wrong-area assignment** (an issue mislabelled with nothing flagging it; a spurious new-area suggestion is visible and mergeable). F-beta: β = 0.5 for the Choice threshold, β = 1 for the Noul; `--conservative` not required for a pilot. All numbers are in `thresholds.json`.
**Trend rules — never on one or two rounds.** The per-round series is descriptive. Secondary descriptive rule: point minimums met in ≥ 3 of the last 4 informative rounds. **Futility stop (R4-13 (5)):** three consecutive informative rounds whose *pooled* paired gain on resolved clusters (`gain_res`) has an 80 % upper bound ≤ +0.02 **and** pooled `P_assign` has an 80 % upper bound below the minimum — then the refinement stops and the frozen rounds are not run (NO-GO, futility); a non-informative refinement round is not counted and only breaks a run of consecutive informative rounds. There is **no futility disjunct on the unresolved rate**: `u` is under the refinement menu's direct control (thresholds, §10 (iv)) and a stop on it would stop what refinement exists to fix; `u` and `gain_all` are reported in the futility table so the per-round critique sees a drift towards the cap. Integrity pauses (leakage, invalid labels, cap breach) are not evidence against HD6 and do not count.
**GO ("some proof it is worthwhile"), evaluated once on the pooled frozen rounds 8–10** (91 scored clusters from 40 PRs planned), under the user's decision of 2026-10-30 (R-051; record in §14): accuracy and the gain over the baseline are judged on the clusters Jev actually answers, abstention has its own cap, and new-area detection is reported but cannot veto. Vocabulary: resolved / abstained / `u` as defined above; the GO population is scored, non-control, no `core_gate`, pooled over rounds 8–10. **All twelve conditions a–l** (`analysis_x2d.go_verdict`; `thresholds.json study.go.all_of`, `x2d.MINIMUMS` and `analysis_x2d.GO` kept equal):
| # | condition | set | kind | value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a | `P_assign` = assigned-correct ÷ assigned | assigned clusters (a subset of resolved; a wrong assignment on a gold-`none` positive counts) | point | ≥ 0.90 |
| b | `P_assign` one-sided 90 % Wilson lower bound | same | bound | ≥ 0.80 |
| c | `R_assign_res` = assigned-correct ÷ (gold-covered ∧ resolved) | resolved covered clusters (a false `none` on a covered cluster counts) | point | ≥ 0.80 |
| d | paired gain `gain_res` = (Jev-correct − B2-correct) ÷ `n_res` | the same resolved clusters for both arms (Jev-abstained clusters are excluded from the pair, not scored wrong for Jev) | point | ≥ +0.10 |
| e | `gain_res` one-sided 90 % PR-cluster-bootstrap lower bound | same (per-PR rows over resolved clusters only) | bound | > 0 |
| f | Jev-correct / B2-wrong resolved clusters from distinct PRs | same | spread | ≥ 3 |
| g | **unresolved rate** `u` = abstained ÷ GO population | all GO-population clusters | point (**the cap**) | ≤ 0.15 |
| h | **abstention guard**: `gain_guess` = (Σ guess-correct − Σ B2-correct) ÷ `n_all`; an abstained cluster (any reason) is scored by the primary arm's own unthresholded combined answer (`none` iff `raw_covered` is false, else `raw_choice`, scored with the rule of §9; a missing raw answer is wrong), a resolved cluster by its composed answer | all GO-population clusters with a B2 outcome | guard: one-sided 95 % PR-cluster-bootstrap lower bound (`GO["guard_level"]` = `thresholds.json study.go.guard_level`) | > 0 |
| i | resolved paired clusters `n_res` | — | data | ≥ 50 |
| j | perception check passes (V-sem's unthresholded combined answer against the labellers', §8; the thresholded agreement and `u` are reported beside it) | §8 sample | other | pass |
| k | N1 at chance | round 0 | other | pass |
| l | every frozen round informative (≥ 20 scored clusters, ≥ 6 PRs, κ ≥ 0.6 on the GO population, labels-check not failed) | per round | other | true |
Removed from the conditions: `P_new`, `R_new` and "real gold-`none` positives ≥ 10". **Precedence (R1-19, unchanged in form):** (1) condition l false (a frozen round not informative, informativeness unmeasured, or its labels-check failed) → **INCONCLUSIVE**, before any point test; (2) any **point** condition (a, c, d, g) false → **NO-GO**; (3) perception or N1 measured and failed → **NO-GO**; (4) all twelve true → **GO**; (5) otherwise (b, e, f, h or i false, or perception or N1 unmeasured) → **INCONCLUSIVE**, `reasons` naming each failed condition (h as "Jev shows no accuracy edge over B2 once its abstentions are answered by its own unthresholded guess (the resolved-cluster gain may be selection)"). **NO-GO** also on futility. A cap breach (g) is a measured product failure (it abstains too often to be worthwhile), hence NO-GO; the guard (h) is an interpretability failure, hence INCONCLUSIVE. **Estimand (R5-03):** a self-aware Jev with no accuracy edge over B2 is treated as a null by council decision, because Scout has no fallback for an unresolved association and X2d changes no design; the guard fails → INCONCLUSIVE, not NO-GO.
**Reported beside the verdict (descriptive; Wilson 95 % intervals; `verdict.json`, `report.md`, the critique pack, RESULTS):** `u` with its per-reason split (`below_t_cov`, `below_t_choice`, `permutation_disagreement`, `failure`) and per round; the **abstention table** (n abstained; B2's accuracy on Jev-abstained clusters, on Jev-resolved clusters and on all: the selection diagnostic, which decides nothing — the guard h does); `gain_guess` with its 95 % bound (the quantity of h) and McNemar; `gain_all` (abstention scored as a miss) with its 90 % bound and McNemar, a report only; the **new-area metrics** `P_new`, `R_new` on real gold-`none` positives (n, gap/new split) and on planted positives separately, each with Wilson 95 % and the sentence "n real positives; no veto on the verdict"; the planted figures (`pooled.planted_new`, R5-07) pool the joint counts of the per-round frozen-round empty-file rename traps (`controls.trap`: `none_correct {k: arm `none` ∧ gold not covered, n: gold not covered}` and `none_n`, the arm's `none` count): `R_new` = Σk ÷ Σn, `P_new` = Σk ÷ Σ`none_n`, each over traps with gold and an arm row (a trap lacking either is `unmeasured`), Wilson 95 %, with `unmeasured` and `source` ("frozen-round empty-file rename traps (one per round); the round-0 planted new concern and the P2 gate are in rounds/0/gates.json"); `R_assign` on all covered clusters (abstention = miss) beside `R_assign_res`; leave-one-round-out figures (R3-15) under the same definitions.
**B2 pairing and abstained clusters, exactly (R4-13 (3), R5-01).** A case enters the resolved pair iff the primary arm's output is `assigned` or `none` and B2 has a scored outcome; `paired` and the per-PR pooling rows have a resolved-only mode, and the rows carry `n_res, d_res, a, ac, gc_res, gc, n_all, d_all, d_guess, abst, abst_b2_correct`; `gain_res` is evaluated on `(d_res, n_res)` for d and e, `gain_guess` on `(d_guess, n_all)` for h (`score_outcome` adds `raw_correct` and `guess_correct`), `gain_all` on `(d_all, n_all)` for the report; the bootstrap resamples PRs as before (B = 10,000, seed `x2d.SEED`). `compose.py` writes `raw_covered` (max area `p_true` ≥ 0.5) and `raw_choice` (argmax `p_true`, ties by area id; None on a failure row) on fan-out rows too, so a fan-out primary has a guess. `summarise` emits `p_assign`, `r_assign_res`, `r_assign`, `gain_res`, `gain_all`, `gain_guess {point, n, lb95, lb90, ci95, jev_only, base_only, mcnemar_p}`, `n_res`, `unresolved {k, n, rate, wilson95, by_reason}`, `abstained`, `real_new`, `planted_new`, `perception`, `n1`, `informative_all`. **Completeness (R5-09):** `verdict()` and `round_results` refuse when any GO-population case lacks a primary-arm row or a B2 outcome, and `verdict()` asserts `gain_guess.n == gain_all.n == unresolved.n == n`. **Report tables (R5-08):** the per-round table is titled "Paired gain vs B2, all clusters (abstention = miss; `gain_all`)"; a primary-arm `gain_res` row (n_res, Jev-only, B2-only, point, McNemar, 95 % CI, lb90, ub80, PRs with Jev-only) and the `gain_guess` row (with lb95) are added, in the report and in the critique pack. The primary arm is V-sem, or the fan-out when K5 fails. A GO licenses a full trial (≥ 2 repositories, dev/test by repository), not adoption.

## 10. Iteration protocol
**Per round r (1–10):** (1) freeze version `v_r` (the question versions, the state builder, the guide, the settings; SHA-256 of each): the guide and the question files of a round are its **own inputs**, `rounds/<r>/inputs/{GUIDE.md,questions/*.json}` (R2-01), copied from the study directory for rounds 0 and 1 and from round r−1's inputs for r ≥ 2 unless the drafter's proposal already sits there; the Jev driver, the labellers and the reference arm read these, and `version.json` hashes them (`guide_version = x2d-association-v1/guide-<first 12 hex of the guide's SHA-256>`, `inputs_sha256`); the study-directory files stay frozen (the v_0 base). `run.sh units R` first checks the live catalogue, state builders, thresholds, round table, model, code and menu against the previous version's refinement envelope (`versioning.py --check`, **every r ≥ 1**, round 1 against round 0; R3-12) before anything is frozen or written. Every later stage of the round refuses when the live inputs, settings, thresholds, state builder, catalogue or code differ from `version.json` (`versioning.py --drift`, R2-03) and re-checks the gates and the previous critique; (2) build units for the round's PRs against the **round-start** catalogue membership (never seen by an arm before); (3) label blind, then `labels-check` (§8), then **gold**; (4) score every arm **once** and save the raw probabilities to `scores.csv` (`case_id, label, score, model, question_version, split, stratum, fallback`); the baselines are replayed per PR after gold exists (§6); `compose` requires the second permutation of `merge.which_area` iff `x2d.flip_selected(cluster_id)` or the run's `flip_all` (recorded in the scores header) **and, when the file holds fan-out rows, an answer or failure row for every area of the run's catalogue for every unit** (`--catalogue`; R3-06): a missing required row gives `unresolved:failure` (a partial fan-out is never composed), and `stage_analysis` refuses while any unit lacks a required row (the driver is resumable; R2-13); `stage_tune` and `stage_analysis` also refuse without their data inputs (`gold.jsonl`, `jev/scores.jsonl`; `arms/B2.jsonl`, `arms/reference.jsonl` and, in rounds 1 and 10, the contrast files; R3-14), and `tune` (r ≥ 1) additionally refuses before writing when `compose.missing_required` over the round's score file, its window units and the round catalogue's area ids is non-empty ("re-run jev R"; presence is not completeness, R4-11); (5) set `t_cov`, `t_choice` with `tune_threshold.py` on the saved scores of rounds `< r` and the round-0 D/P units only (stratified 5-fold; `--repeats 5 --conservative` under 50 cases per label; provisional 0.7 under 30; never 0.5) and apply them to round r. Tuning rows: window units only for rounds ≥ 1 (D and P for round 0; never audit units or controls); Choice rows cover **all** gold-labelled clusters whose Noul passed `t_cov` (gold-`none` clusters included) **for which every required permutation was answered**, label 1 iff all asked permutations agree **and** the choice ∈ acceptable, score = mean confidence; recall is over gold-covered. After tuning the **composed rule** is evaluated on the dev rows at the tuned pair under the §9 definitions (R4-13 (4)): dev `P_assign` and `R_assign_res` against the minimums **and the dev unresolved rate ≤ 0.15** are enforced; dev `P_new`, `R_new` are recorded, not enforced; if any enforced figure fails the decision is `no_threshold_meets_minimums` with provisional 0.7 (without the unresolved-rate term the F0.5 Choice tuning would buy precision with abstention and walk the frozen rounds into the cap). **Before round 8** `run.sh rescore-v7` (requires the round-7 critique; v_7 frozen) re-scores rounds 0–7 under **round 7's inputs, settings and model** (arms `sem,fan`; round 0: the manifest `main` units restricted to D ∪ P; rounds 1–7: the window units) into `rounds/<rr>/jev-v7/scores.jsonl` (development only, never fresh evidence; a killed run resumes, a finished one never re-runs). **Each unit's `state.unit.excerpt` is re-rendered under round 7's `excerpt_lines`** from the same member text at the unit's commit (same representative-member rule; ids, membership and gold unchanged) into `rounds/<rr>/jev-v7/units.jsonl`, whose rows carry `excerpt_lines` and whose digest is the header's input digest (`input_sha256`, which the consumer compares with the SHA-256 of the units file; R3-05, R4-06); **the re-score is charged to the $20 total, not to the round caps** (R3-17; $10 until rev 11, R-052). `stage_units 8` and `tune.py --round 8` refuse unless all eight files exist, every header's `model` and catalogue digest equal those of `rounds/7/jev/scores.jsonl`, its `versions` are **exactly** the three question versions `merge.covered`, `merge.which_area`, `merge.area_covers` **each equal to round 7's**, the header's `input_sha256` equals the SHA-256 of `jev-v7/units.jsonl` (R4-06; one changed byte is refused and nothing is written), the units rows carry round 7's `excerpt_lines`, and `compose.missing_required` over the round's dev units (every `merge.covered` row, every required `merge.which_area` permutation, every area of the round's catalogue for the fan-out) is empty (R3-18). Tuning runs once on those `v_7` rows and rounds 9 and 10 reuse it (recorded in the round-7 critique); `question_version` is kept in `scores.csv`. The SHA-256 of `tune_threshold.py` is pinned in `signoff.json` and checked by `verify_pins`. (6) the **council critique** (below); (7) `v_{r+1}`. Earlier rounds may be re-scored under `v_{r+1}` for development comparison only; never as fresh evidence. Rounds 8–10 use `v_7` unchanged and are scored once each; they never feed tuning.
**Teacher-forced primary, at round granularity** (deviation from "after each PR", §14): units of a round are built against the membership at the round's start, which absorbed the *gold* primary assignments of rounds `< r` (gold `none` goes to the pool; a pool area is minted untitled once ≥ 3 pooled files share a new leaf directory and is never a Choice option); absorption is chronological (R1-11): commits are applied in round order, each commit's inherit operations before its units' gold, so a path deleted and re-created within a round is handled. `gold.py` computes every gold file and the next membership **in memory first** and writes only when every unit the file must cover has usable gold — the window units for r ≥ 1 (round 10 and the contrast tag included), the D and P units and **every non-P2 control of `units.jsonl`** for round 0 (a P2 control derives its gold from its source; a control the cap never reached is not dropped silently, R4-07), the labelled contrast set for the contrast tag (R3-03, independent of whether a next membership is built); otherwise it exits, writing nothing, naming the cases and `label_x2d --round r [--tag contrast] --new-round <name>` (a capped case or an unparsable adjudication); the baseline replay and the divergence then report `unmeasured` with that reason (R2-04). `gold.py` itself refuses to overwrite an existing file, so `run.sh gold` and `gates-label` call it for a file only when that file is absent or empty (the main gold, the contrast gold and round 0's two files each separately), echoing "exists (append-only): skipped": a contrast gold completed after the main gold exists succeeds (R4-01). Gold selects the label rows of the round's **current guide version**, the last usable final row winning. A unit is independent of any arm's earlier mistakes and one label set serves all arms; baselines are replayed per PR (§6). **Free-running secondary:** each arm carries its own membership (absorbing its own assignments at the end of each round); the report gives per-round **replayed free-running divergence** from the gold membership for each Jev arm and B2 (file-set Jaccard per area, persisting wrong memberships).
**Refinement envelope (it scopes rule 5; it cannot waive it).** *Permissible per-round changes (the menu):* (i) question wording and criteria text of the questions of §7 (in the round's `inputs/questions`); (ii) state fields chosen from the menu — sample files per area 3–8 (`sample_files[:n]`), excerpt 30–120 lines, V-sib on/off, `include_pr_title` on/off (removes `state.pr.title` from V-sem and V-sib; the V-title arm is always on), field order; (iii) option presentation (order rule within `permutation:unit_id`/`fixed`); (iv) thresholds from prior rounds' scores, under the composed-rule dev check of step (5), which enforces dev `P_assign`, `R_assign_res` and the dev unresolved rate ≤ 0.15 (R4-13 (4)); when the tuned decisions miss the dev cap and the decisions revert to the provisional 0.7, the round's `rounds/<r>/thresholds.json` records `composed_dev_provisional` (the same figures at the provisional decisions) beside `composed_dev` (R5-11); no decision changes; (v) guide clarifications derived from labeller disagreement **without seeing that round's Jev scores** (rounds ≤ 7 only, in the round's `inputs/GUIDE.md`), **at most one per round (C ≤ 1; `versioning.clarify` refuses a second revision)**: `run.sh clarify R "reason"` (before `jev/scores.jsonl` exists; it also refuses after gold) first checks that relabelling the round's wide cases (and its contrast sample) fits the session cap computed with C + 1 (§12) and **refuses before any revision is recorded** when it does not (`label_x2d.py clarify_precheck`, run by `run.sh clarify-precheck` and again by `clarify`, then writes `rounds/R/clarify-refused.json` = `{utc, reason, cap, used, planned}`, create-once, and exits non-zero; the file is treated like a recorded revision, so the round proceeds scored and, if κ still fails, non-informative; R4-02, R5-12). **Order (R5-05):** run `run.sh clarify-precheck R` (rounds 1–7; `label_x2d.py --round R --precheck-clarify` alone) **before** editing `rounds/R/inputs/GUIDE.md`: a refusal means no edit. **Recovery:** if the guide was edited before a refusal, restore the bytes whose SHA-256 is `guide_sha256` of `rounds/R/version.json`: `rounds/<R-1>/inputs/GUIDE.md` (round 1: the study `GUIDE.md`) when round R's inputs were copied from it, otherwise the proposal approved by `next_inputs_sha256` in `reviews/pilot-round-<R-1>.md` (its versioned diff is in that critique); `require_version R` must pass before any later stage; keep the refused text under `reviews/`. If the pre-check passes, the clarification continues: it records `{utc, guide_sha256, reason}` in `rounds/R/guide-revisions.jsonl`, relabels every wide case of round R (and its contrast sample) under the new `guide_version` and re-runs `labels-check` (a still-failing κ after the clarification lets the round proceed scored and non-informative, §8). A round whose wide labels cannot be completed within its cap even so (also after a recorded clarification) is an integrity pause "cap breach" (§8): reported, decided by the council and the user, not evidence against HD6 and not counted towards futility. Clarification is **prospective only**: earlier rounds' gold and memberships are never rewritten; affected earlier units may be relabelled for the record (`label_x2d --round q --new-round NAME --cases IDS --guide-round R`), **charged to round q's session cap and skipped (the pre-check refuses) when that cap does not allow them**, reported in the critique, never absorbed. *Each such change ships as a versioned diff with digests and is reviewed in the per-round critique, which approves the **next round's inputs by digest** (`next_inputs_sha256`; for round 7, round 7's own inputs, v_7 frozen) and is the zero-issue review round for it* (`reviews/pilot-round-<r>.md`); the run does not continue to round r+1 until it reports zero issues. ## 13. Threats
- **Few, lumpy, test-heavy units.** 91 frozen clusters from 40 PRs (rev 1: 63 / 32 before the fold); before the fold 62 % of the 221 scored clusters were tests and 9 were core; B2's test-mirror rule may already be near-perfect (K2); a GO needs a +0.10 paired gain over a baseline that may be at 0.8 or higher, so with 91 clusters Jev ≳ 0.93 and B2 ≲ 0.83. Power is stated in §11 before spend. One repository: results do not transfer; the next study needs ≥ 2.
- **Episode dominance:** the PR cap bounds one PR's share (largest scored share 0.435, round 9; round 10 after the fold 0.167); sensitivity by episode (#14569, #14358, #14168, #16403, #14652) is reported.
- **Labels are agent labels** (R-039, `agent_reviewed`) from the families of the mapper (codex) and a different one (claude); Jev and the labellers answer the same questions from nearly the same text: agreement is an internal-consistency result, not proof. The wide-evidence labellers see more than Jev; the perception check measures the gap.
- **Title leakage and path leakage:** V-title measures the first; N1/P1 the second.
- **Scope shards cut concerns** that span two shards; the catalogue is a function of the shard boundary (topics are alphabetical). The scope draws cover `docs_src` and `tests` by topic, but a topic's area cannot absorb files of another shard.
- **Catalogue ageing** across 22 months: gold-`none` rate per round (gap vs new) is the monitor; the paired baseline absorbs difficulty.
- **Round-granular teacher forcing:** units and labels of a round are built on the round-start membership, so later PRs' siblings are missing at build time; the baselines are replayed per PR against the membership at each unit's parent commit (§6) and the round-start B2 is a reported sensitivity; Jev reads no ownership.
- **Related-migration episodes** (#14168 Pydantic v1-in-v2, #16403 OpenTelemetry, #15030 SSE): examples + core + tests arrive together; the PR cap and the by-episode report limit their influence.
- **Shared families, model drift:** the mapper (codex luna), the labeller (codex) and Jev's pin; names may roll; probes bracket each collection but cannot see a same-name change.
- **Long state (≥ 40 options)** is a 1.13 weak spot; the 1.14 jaggedness page was unavailable to X2c and is re-checked.
- **The extension is not the product:** the product `Reconcile` is destructive to membership layers; a GO cannot ship without a design round (§1).
- **A single refinement path:** seven rounds on one chronological series with the same labellers; the frozen rounds are scored once, the skill's "fresh replay window"; if the frozen rounds were seen before the final verdict (integrity pause), a newer window is required.
- **Abstention gaming / selective abstention (R4-13, R5-01, R5-03):** with a separate cap on abstention and the gain judged on the clusters Jev resolves, a model can raise its resolved-cluster accuracy and gain by abstaining where it would be wrong or where B2 is right. Controls: the unresolved-rate cap g (`u ≤ 0.15`, a point condition), the guard h (the one-sided 95 % PR-cluster-bootstrap lower bound of `gain_guess` > 0, where abstained clusters are answered by Jev's own unthresholded guess, so selection moves where Jev abstains but never what its answer would have been), the abstention table (B2's accuracy on abstained vs resolved clusters, reported) and the `gain_all` report, the binding nulls N4a, N4b, N4b-cap and N4b-ρ0 (§11; GO ≤ 0.10 in every cell) and the dev unresolved-rate term of the tuning check (§10 (5)). **Estimand (R5-03):** a self-aware Jev with no accuracy edge over B2 is treated as a null by council decision, because Scout has no fallback for an unresolved association and X2d changes no design; the guard fails → INCONCLUSIVE, not NO-GO. Residual risk: abstention correlated with difficulty at a rate below the cap still raises `gain_res` somewhat; h bounds the effect through the abstained clusters' own guesses, so the reported `gain_guess`, `gain_all` and the abstention table are read with the verdict.
- **New-area detection is not powered:** about 10 gold-`none` positives (`real_none_proxy`) cannot veto and cannot support a claim; `P_new` / `R_new` are reported with Wilson 95 % intervals and the P2 gate (§5) is the only planted test (UNMEASURED below 8 gap positives; `RESULTS.md` then states "R_new on catalogue gaps has no planted test").
