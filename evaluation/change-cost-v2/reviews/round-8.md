Nit — `DESIGN-NOTES.md:66` labels raw active-hour quantiles over all 7,717 panel PRs as `Y_active` statistics next to the 7,030-row `Y_active` count.** Text: "Y_active: median 1.0 h, p75 2.3 h, p95 8.4 h; single-commit 42 %; floor share over non-missing rows 38.2 % (n = 7,030; …)". The hour figures are `constructs.json` `overall.ALL.active_hours_q` (0.5: 1.0, 0.75: 2.2761, 0.95: 8.4461, `constructs.json:1237-1242`), computed by `constructs.py:53` on the unmasked `active_hours` column of every panel row (7,717), including django, whose `y_active` is set missing by the per-repository floor rule (`build_panel.py:169-173` masks `y_active`/`_g2`/`_g8` only). The sentence therefore attributes pre-exclusion quantiles to the gated primary outcome (experiment rule 6: numbers must not be misdescribed). Design and code are unaffected; not in `PREREGISTRATION.md`; not previously reviewed (no round mentions `active_hours`). *Fix (text-only, either):* (A) "active hours over all 7,717 panel PRs (before django's `Y_active` masking): median 1.0 h, p75 2.3 h, p95 8.4 h; `Y_active` over the 7,030 non-missing rows: 1.0 h / 2.5 h / 8.9 h; single-commit 42 %; floor share 38.2 %"; or (B) replace the three hour figures by the 7,030-row values 1.0 / 2.5 / 8.9 h.
