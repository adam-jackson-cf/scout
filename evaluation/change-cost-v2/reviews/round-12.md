Severity minor: the route is not observed on the pinned endpoint (run-1 diagnostic at 2,000 tokens returned `finish_reason=stop`; the 95 run-1 rows were all `length`), but the amendment's own principle and round 11's #1 standard ("sibling infrastructure path") apply. Raised by the challenger; adopted.
Required fix (a): `answer()` aborts unless `finish_reason == "stop"` (message names the annotator and the finish reason; "return to council"); extend the `tests.py:292-297` check with a `content_filter`/`None` finish-reason case on the real `annotate.answer`; update `DESIGN-NOTES.md` §8 wording if the count changes and §10 digests for `annotate.py`/`tests.py`; AMENDMENT-1 §4 bullet 2 to read "truncated (`finish_reason != "stop"`) or empty".
