### Safety census

Blind-label every Jev-below-floor case plus original audit draws, deduplicate and globally shuffle the union. Only original audit draws enter retrieval/threshold-transfer estimators at stored π; safety-only labels never change audit inclusion probabilities.

Independent questions: truth ∈ {true,false,cannot_tell}; critical mechanism ∈ {yes,no,cannot_tell}. Critical means visible reachable RCE, privilege escalation, irreversible data loss or broad service outage without visible guard. **No critical mechanism visible → no**. Critical cannot_tell means a visible mechanism with ambiguous reach/guard, not generic whole-program uncertainty.

Below-floor predicate:
1. **Violating:** truth=true and (scanner severity critical OR critical=yes).
2. **Cleared:** truth=false OR (critical=no AND scanner severity not critical).
3. **Unresolved:** otherwise.

Missing criticality defaults unknown; malformed values refused. Unknown truth can be cleared by explicit noncritical evidence and noncritical scanner severity. Any violating sampled case is an absolute veto, unless delivery/global integrity already failed. Unresolved safety blocks support, not a decisive valid retrieval negative. Baseline-bottom violating counts are descriptive on the labelled union, with floor-overlap/routes/unlabelled baseline-bottom count disclosed, never a symmetric baseline census.

## 6. HT estimators and studentised paired run-ratio interval

Run inclusion q_r=20/L_r is constant within repository. Conditional audit inclusion π_i comes from the product row. Population HT totals use **1/(q_rπ_i)**: T_r=ΣY/(qπ), C_Ar=ΣYI_A/(qπ). Ratios are not unbiased merely because their totals are HT. Report raw/weighted counts, both recalls, D_r/D, unmeasured and inclusion stages.

Primary: **studentised (percentile-t) paired run-ratio bootstrap**, selected by the predeclared revision-4 diagnostic rule (§8), not an automatic fallback. Keep the McCarthy–Snowden m = G−1 structure (R-071/R-072): G = 20 sampled runs per repository, m = 19 replacement draws, B = 1,999, seed 169, common draws across arms/vertices and independent repository streams. Canonical UTF-8 bytewise (repository, run, issue) order; no FPC.

Let A_i = J_i−B_i and D̂_r = ΣA_i/ΣT_i for G run totals. Residual e_i = A_i−D̂_r T_i. Use the no-FPC run-linearised variance **V_r = G Σ(e_i−ē)² / [(G−1)(ΣT_i)²]**, and **V = ΣV_r/9** for the equal-repository mean. Recompute these on each m-run resample. Pivot t* = (D*−D̂)/sqrt(V*). A zero-variance replicate with |D*−D̂| ≤ 1e−14 has pivot 0; otherwise retain the signed infinity, without counting it omitted. Undefined variance and zero-positive denominators are omitted. More than 5% omitted replicates is inadequate; no retained pivot or undefined original variance yields unmeasured/inadequate, not another method. Infinite selected quantiles yield the corresponding [−1,1]-clipped endpoint.

Quantile indices use retained B′: floor(.025B′), ceil(.975B′)−1, zero-based. **Do not clip pivots.** Interval = [D̂−q_.975 sqrt(V), D̂−q_.025 sqrt(V)], then clip endpoints to [−1,1]. The historical percentile method remains diagnostic only. Published resampling rationale is not a proof of ratio coverage; the locked-inventory gate remains binding.

### Complete-label cells and populations

Outer design: generate a finite population on **every eligible run's locked size**, sample 20 runs per repository without replacement, then product Bernoulli audit and paired bootstrap. Truth is the full-label equal-repository population gain, not the selected frame gain.

Coverage cells are the full Cartesian product: M ∈ **{2,000,1,500,1,000,850,700,550,400}**; prevalence {.10,.20,.40,.55}; latent gain SD {0,.10}; requested gains {0,.05,.08,.10,.12,.20}; positive-key overlap {0,.5}; failures {0,.02}; scanner-control populations {false,true}; below-k rank structure {uniform,linear-gradient}; size dependence {0,.05,.20}. **16,128 cells. Safety settings do not multiply coverage cells.**

Positive keys share with probability ρ, otherwise independent, allowing both discordance directions. Baseline recall uses feasibility-conditioned normal deviations; report realised truth, never pretend an unbounded normal recall is possible. Jev annotations are the baseline head M minus failures; the deterministic remainder is unchanged.

Uniform below-k order is the historical assumption. For linear gradient, below-k slot count H = N−k and remaining positive count P give p = P/H and a = min(p,1−p). Slot i has target positive density **p + a[1−(2i+1)/H]**, i = 0,…,H−1. A single uniform shift of cumulative probabilities assigns exactly P positive slots; shuffle identities within positive/negative slots. This defines declining density and a feasible exact count, not merely triangular keys whose rank mixture is nonlinear.

The size factor subtracts **a_size × (N−N_min)/(N_max−N_min)** from requested gain, zero for constant sizes. N_min/N_max span **all eligible runs of all three locked repositories**, giving one offset per size. Coverage uses a_size ∈ {0,.05,.20}; references/safety retain {0,.05}, since .20 may make calibrated .20 unreachable at prevalence .55. Earlier .05 timing is author-recorded, not independently verified.

Each cell has **five independent populations × 2,000 eligible trials = 10,000**. Seeds use manifest HMAC over canonical cell/population/attempt/phase, no cross-cell common seeds. ## 12. Threats and interpretation

Purposive repositories, first-occurrence introduced/regressed restriction and 20–2,000-issue eligibility favour bulk refactors/vendor/generated baselines. Merge/squash topology changes run meaning. SRS gives run-stage probability inference only within fixed eligible envelopes; it does not license future-commit/project generalisation. Large runs/rule/path roles can dominate pooled recall; sensitivity lines disclose rather than erase this.

Twenty clusters and ratio/nonlinear missing envelopes can undercover; citations and finite fixtures do not replace the binding outer one-sided gate. Omitting FPC is intended conservative, not a coverage proof. Conditional audit-only census bounds may be deterministic; do not treat copies as independent empirical confirmation of run uncertainty.

Agent/Jev shared question, facts/path role/tool signal and training exposure can create common-mode judgement; hidden severity does not remove it. Construct is bounded-state blind-agent truth, not whole-program defects. All three labeller/adjudicator cutoffs overlap the window; Jev unknown. Disappearance is a noisy churn proxy.

Recall ceiling depends on k/prevalence. Product cap order and delivery failures can remove reorderable mass; undeclared study stops cannot be product failure. Absolute safety can veto a useful ranker, and a relevant unresolved case blocks support. Futility/OC projections expose reachability without relaxing REQ-169. Baseline safety is incomplete outside labelled overlap. No calibrated score, tuned **0.30**, blocking lane, current `--rank` flag or repository-wide no-miss claim follows.

Development-to-test transfer of unresolved rates, durations and costs can fail despite conservative reservations. Unequal per-commit inclusion under the greedy five-unit cap yields a commit-balanced dev mixture, not a unit-uniform estimate; the dev windows end in 2023 versus test histories from 2024 onward. **Within-commit dependence** remains despite the five-unit cap and ≥30 distinct development commits/repository; Wilson and Maurer–Pontil independence assumptions may fail, and cluster resampling does not prove transportability. OC validity is limited to registered generator ranges. Size-dependent cells showed positive point bias about +.0018 to +.0027 in the synthetic pilot; report it rather than treating HT ratios as unbiased. Revision-6 to revision-7 measured CPU drift was +66.2% construction/unit, +30.3% re-evaluation/unit, +31.3% gate check and +8.1% complete trial; host load is a possible cause [INFERENCE], not an established explanation. The fixed one-run/integrity-only-rerun/maximum-input rule prevents timing selection. Eight-worker scaling, distinct-size enumeration costs and projected memory require locked-size remeasurement; absent capacity/fit fails closed. Subscription quotas may prevent up to about 150,000 sessions despite projections; inventory cannot establish provider capacity, and quota pauses consume the fixed clock.
