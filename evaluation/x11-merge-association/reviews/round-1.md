## Council synthesis

### 1. Recommendation
Return the draft for a rev 2 that fixes R1-01 to R1-30 together with the implementation. Then hold an implementation review round on the code, the GUIDE, the staged manifests and the validation records. The core design is sound and should be kept: three repositories (one picked by a model-free proxy, one neither Python nor Rust), two catalogue ages, three arms, closed-loop replay without teacher forcing, a no-action maintainer policy, labellers other than Terra, and per-merge-cap clusters counted as unmeasured. Every REQ-176 element is present in some form. The defects are in how endpoints are defined, how the verdict is reached, how the freeze is staged and how the work is sized.

### 2. Alternatives
- **A. Revise in place (recommended).** Keeps a single study that covers REQ-176. The cost is a heavier rev 2.
- **B. Split into X11a (accuracy and verdict) and X11b (guard and recovery, a descriptive case series).** This isolates the decision from endpoints that have at most 6 events. The cost is two council tracks. REQ-176 names both parts but does not forbid separate preregistrations. Worth considering if R1-03, R1-15 and R1-16 cannot be made rigorous at this event count.
- **C. Lower-bound or three-zone decision rule (R1-05).** Fewer false adoptions near 0.80, at the price of a larger n or a higher chance of an inconclusive result. Keeping the point-estimate bar is allowed under REQ-177 only if its operating characteristics are stated and accepted.
- **D. Fixed-length primary segment per repository and age (R1-17/R1-18).** Comparable cells and bounded cost, but it gives up long-horizon decay, which the elapsed-age reporting would still describe.

### 3. Evidence

- Exposure: `evaluation/change-cost-v2/pins.json:16–21,72–77`; `change-cost-v2/PREREGISTRATION.md:30–32,52–54`. A search found no Django, TypeScript, gin or cobra material in the X2d or post-run folders.
- Calculations: binomial window dips (R1-03, computed here). Operating characteristics and DEFF half-widths (R1-05, R1-21) come from the experiment-peer's normal approximation with continuity correction, which I checked arithmetically. Budget arithmetic is internally consistent: 3×$15+$5 = $50; 9,600×3 = 28,800 ≤ 30,000 sessions; maps 6+6+6 = 18 ≤ 24. Only the wall-time ceiling is inconsistent (R1-18).

### 4. Risks
- **Supply:** Django, TypeScript or the selected Go repository may not reach ≥200 assignments per arm and age. That would make HD8 inconclusive regardless of quality (R1-18, R1-19); actual supply is unmeasured.
- **Label validity:** agent gold has unknown agreed-error, and the OpenAI family overlaps across mapper, fallback and labeller (R1-12).
- **Catalogue draw variance:** six single map draws, outside every interval (R1-22).
- **Guard and recovery:** these may stay uninformative even after the fixes (at most 6 events per arm); alternative B reduces the exposure.
- **Reversibility:** nothing has run, so every fix is a document or code change. Once real labels exist, the window and sample cannot be extended (l.21), so supply decisions must be settled before freeze.

### 5. Execution outline
1. Build the driver and stages from §11. Add a separate post-branch label stage, and make every stage refuse to run without its prerequisites (R1-01, R1-02).
2. Write and authorise the model-free `inventory` script. Run it only after council review of its digest. Record U/N, supply, role and extension distributions and exposure intersections in a review round, with no labels or associations (R1-02, R1-18, R1-19, R1-23).
3. Using the inventory, fix the proxy eligibility rule, the window-end rule, the processing order and the budgets (R1-18, R1-19).
4. Rewrite §6 with statistical fall definitions, a warning horizon, a shared guard/precision merge index, a coverage reference and recovery as a contrast (R1-03, R1-14, R1-15, R1-16).
5. Rewrite §5 and §7: scoring and combination rules, the outcome table, the concern-grouping pass, the agreed-case audit, the κ gate and the gap/new evidence method (R1-09–R1-12, R1-27).
6. Rewrite §8: REQ-102 losses, an ordered decision table, unmeasured caps, the stated estimand and operating characteristics (R1-05–R1-08, R1-17).
7. Rewrite §9 validation: binding clustered boundary scenarios, stochastic guard nulls, fixtures for every decision branch and every new endpoint (R1-04, R1-20, R1-26).
8. Settle the PR-metadata position and the minor items (R1-13, R1-21–R1-30). Fill the protocol manifest, compute SHA-256s and submit rev 2 for round 2.
