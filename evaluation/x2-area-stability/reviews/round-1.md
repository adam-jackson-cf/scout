**8. Contiguity gate, edge-sitting `t*`, and an unsupported direction claim.**
Location: prereg §4 step 2, §10 "Pairwise, not chained replay … `[INFERENCE: lower]`", DRAFT choices 6–7; `analysis.py:155-160`. Evidence: the spurious rate is a ratio of nested counts; raising `t` removes `same` and `different` matches alike, so the point rate oscillates around 5 % by single matches (e.g. 4/80 pass, 4/75 fail, 3/60 pass) and a non-contiguous pass set is an *expected* outcome of sampling noise, not a pathology — yet it yields `inconclusive` and wastes the study. `t* = max(pass)` sits by construction at the survival edge (S1 and S4 both adopt at survival exactly 0.80), and C1 has no precision guard. Production `Reconcile` keeps unmatched areas active (`areas.go:154-165` `Kept`), so multi-refresh behaviour is not "lower" by any argument given. Fix: (a) drop contiguity as a verdict condition; `t* = max(pass)`; non-contiguity is reported as a flag in RESULTS and must be stated in any design proposal; (b) add a label-free **chained survival** secondary measure to the replay: fold F1→R1→R2→M1→M2 through `Reconcile` with accumulating areas at every grid `t`, report per-step matched/active-prior, created, kept, retired, and the 4-step share of F1 areas matched at every step; reported at `t*`, not gating; (c) replace the direction claim with "unknown; see chained survival"; (d) §4 states the rationale for `max(pass)`: a spurious merge mislabels persistently, a miss only mints a new area.

## 8. Alternatives considered

- *Sign off with the production-only stratum and fix the text only.* Rejected: it keeps a 4-transition estimand for a reason shown to be false and discards two exchangeable refreshes.

## 9. Risks (recorded, not issues)

- Two clusters, one run each; shared prior maps (F1 in three transitions; R1, R2, M1 in two) make the effective number of refreshes below the nominal count.
- Clawpatch's inventory may include `.scout/` in production (issue 14); X2 measures the clean configuration.
