## Procedure (`AGENTS.md` design rules 1–2)

1. The main agent copies the base to `scout-design.base.md`, writes `scout-design.proposed.md` (revision 2.2) and `proposed.diff`, and reports their SHA-256 values.
2. Round N: an innovation council session plus an independent challenger from a different model family (both through `claude -p` / `codex exec` per the model-use rule) review the proposed design against the base, this proposal, STATUS §6, R-036/R-037, `inertia-bench/docs/design/05-harness-design.md:879-920` and the AGENTS draft. Output: `round-<N>.md` with issues, severity, and verdict. Fixes go in `round-<N>-response.md`.
3. Iterate until a round reports **zero issues**. The final round names the proposed file's SHA-256. The main agent then replaces `docs/scout-design.md` only if `shasum -a 256` matches.
