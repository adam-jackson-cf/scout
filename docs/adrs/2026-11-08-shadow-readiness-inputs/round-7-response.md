## Delegated consolidations

The agent adopts the recommended late-evidence lifecycle, with these conservative consolidations under R-064:

1. **One lifecycle, no drop/retroactive-insert branch.** Collection, merge and import preserve late facts, attempts, links and events; trusted publication stamps them once, gaps their exposure points, discharges their covered completeness obligations and admits their events to both cohort-wide vetoes. Local late input is pending until that publication. ADR-012 and REQ-78 state the lifecycle; other mirrors reference it instead of maintaining variants.
2. **No ready result while trusted input is pending.** current_window refuses certification as unmeasured/exit 2 while trusted readiness input is pending or stamped after C. Explicit historical reproduction remains frozen and cannot certify current readiness. This is deliberately stricter than disclosure alone: a stuck frontier or delayed publication must not leave an old ready result usable as current evidence.
3. **One rule for imported history entering a sealed ledger.** Freshly stamp every new imported history row, not only rows originally at/before C; retain original provenance, deduplicate action IDs and verify original authorization. If cross-lineage verdict order cannot be proved, refuse readiness until a new authorized maintainer verdict resolves it. No guessed ordering or silent overwrite.
4. **Proof or refusal, not fallbacks.** Unproven eligibility, binding, clocks or completeness cannot certify coverage. Unverifiable numeric attempt ordering refuses readiness; unsupported canonical encoding refuses reproduction rather than silently re-encoding historical evidence. Unknown cancellation/skip causes are assessed or gapped, not presumed exclusions.

These are conservative definitions and certification invariants, not new commands, workflows, transports, storage recipes or resolution APIs. Requirement-changing wiring still needs its own adopted zero-issue ADR.
