# ADR-014: Capture X1 delegated experiment decisions

- **Status:** accepted; decided by the agent under owner delegation (R-064)
- **Date:** 2026-11-08
- **Scope:** X1 protocol capture under REQ-169; no product adoption or relation to an earlier ADR.

## Decision

Capture the recommended X1 choices under **R-064 as landed with ADR-011**. REQ-169 delegates the experiment's k, floor, sampling, interval and capped/failed-unit handling to its council-signed preregistration. This ADR is the owner's requested capture mechanism, not an independent grant of experiment authority.

The choices below govern [X1 revision 9](../../../evaluation/x1-triage-ranking/PREREGISTRATION.md) and its [sealed OC contract](../../../evaluation/x1-triage-ranking/oc-manifest.json). The README links here without a second decision table. No product default, flag, threshold, sampler constant or accepted ADR is changed. Separate signed ADRs are required for deterministic-default adoption, implementing `--rank jev`, product sampler parameters and D1-R set-or-replace/minimum review. Route-only is not reopened.
